## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Session Switcher — practice from any checkpoint
- Show Hitboxes — visualize player zones
- Replay Notes — record and review match runs
- Playback Speed — adjust playback for study (0.1x to 5x)

### Wallhack
- Kit Catalog — browse kits, colors, badges, celebrations
- Custom Skin Notes — organize player cosmetics
- Camera Trail — improved camera visibility

### Aimbot

- Player Switch Control — manually switch players mid-match
- Input Buffer Notes — chain complex maneuvers in the editor
